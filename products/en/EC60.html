Maintenance Tips for the Volvo EC60

To get the most out of your Volvo EC60, regular maintenance is essential. Below are some maintenance tips that can help extend the life of your machine and ensure it operates at peak performance.

  • Engine Care: Keep the engine oil at the recommended level and change the oil and filters regularly. Inspect the air filters to ensure optimal engine performance and replace them when necessary.

  • Hydraulic System: Regularly check the hydraulic fluid levels and inspect for any leaks. Keeping the system clean and well-maintained ensures that it continues to perform efficiently.

  • Track and Undercarriage Maintenance: Inspect the tracks regularly for wear and ensure they are properly tensioned. Clean the undercarriage to prevent debris buildup that could affect performance.

  • Safety Inspections: Regularly check safety features such as ROPS and FOPS, along with the condition of the cab. Ensuring that the cab remains free of debris and is properly maintained is critical for operator safety.
