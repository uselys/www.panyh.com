Maintenance and Care for the Kobelco SK350 Excavator

Proper maintenance is essential to ensuring the long-term reliability and performance of your refurbished Kobelco SK350. Below are some key maintenance tips:

Hydraulic System Maintenance

  • Regularly inspect and replace hydraulic filters to ensure the smooth operation of the hydraulic system.

  • Check for leaks in hoses and seals to prevent hydraulic fluid loss, which can reduce efficiency.

  • Clean and lubricate the hydraulic components to reduce wear and extend their lifespan.

Engine Maintenance

  • Follow recommended oil change intervals, and always use high-quality engine oil.

  • Inspect and replace air filters to ensure that the engine receives clean air.

  • Monitor engine temperatures and coolant levels to prevent overheating and engine damage.

Track and Undercarriage Maintenance

  • Regularly inspect the undercarriage for signs of wear, especially the tracks, rollers, and sprockets.

  • Clean the undercarriage to prevent dirt and debris from affecting the performance and longevity of the components.

  • Check the track tension and adjust it as needed to avoid excessive wear and improve fuel efficiency.

Cabin and Safety Features

  • Ensure that all safety systems, such as the backup alarm and emergency lights, are in proper working order.

  • Clean and maintain the operator cabin, ensuring that windows, seat belts, and controls are functioning properly.
