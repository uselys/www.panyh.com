Refurbished SANY SY265 Excavator

SANY Heavy Industry’s rise from a domestic Chinese manufacturer to a global powerhouse is one of the most notable industrial success stories of the 21st century. By 2025, SANY had become the world’s largest excavator producer by volume, with over 100,000 units sold annually. The SY265 series, introduced in the early 2010s, was designed to fill the gap between mid-size and large excavators, offering high breakout force, fuel efficiency, and adaptability for diverse job sites.

  • Track Width: 600 mm standard
  • Ground Clearance: ~470 mm

 

Engine and Powertrain

  • Engine Model: Cummins QSB6.7 or SANY-branded turbo diesel
  • Rated Power Output: 190–200 hp (140–149 kW)
  • Fuel Tank Capacity: ~465 liters
  • Gradeability: Up to 35°
  • Travel Speed: 3.5–5.3 km/h
  • Swing Speed: ~11 rpm

 

Hydraulic System

  • Main Pump Type: Variable displacement axial piston pump

  • Operating Pressure: Up to 34.3 MPa
  • Hydraulic Oil Capacity: ~277 liters
  • Boom Cylinder Bore: ~135 mm
  • Arm Cylinder Bore: ~120 mm
  • Bucket Cylinder Bore: ~110 mm

 

Performance Metrics

  • Max Digging Depth: ~6.7 meters

  • Max Dump Height: ~7.2 meters
  • Tear-Out Force: ~227 kN

 

Cab and Operator Features

Smart Features and Efficiency

Attachments Compatibility
