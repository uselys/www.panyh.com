Maintenance Tips for the Komatsu PC128

To get the most out of your Komatsu PC128, proper maintenance is crucial. Below are some tips for keeping the machine running at its best:

  • Hydraulic System: Regularly check the hydraulic fluid levels and replace filters to ensure that the hydraulic system remains efficient and free of contaminants.

  • Engine Care: Monitor oil levels, coolant temperatures, and replace air filters as necessary to keep the engine in top shape.
