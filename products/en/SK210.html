Maintenance and Care for the Kobelco SK210 Excavator

Proper maintenance is crucial to ensure that your Kobelco SK210 continues to perform at its best over the years. Here are some essential maintenance tips for the SK210:

Engine Maintenance

  • Follow the recommended oil change intervals and use high-quality oil to ensure the engine operates efficiently.

  • Inspect and replace air filters regularly to maintain optimal engine performance.

  • Monitor coolant levels and check for leaks to prevent engine overheating.

Hydraulic System Maintenance

  • Regularly check hydraulic fluid levels and ensure that there are no leaks in the hydraulic lines and seals.

  • Replace hydraulic filters as part of routine maintenance to prevent blockages and maintain system efficiency.

  • Clean and lubricate hydraulic components to reduce wear.

Track and Undercarriage Maintenance

  • Inspect the tracks, rollers, and sprockets for wear and tear regularly. Adjust the track tension as needed to avoid excessive wear.

  • Clean the undercarriage of dirt, mud, and debris to prevent damage to the components.

  • Lubricate the undercarriage to ensure smooth movement and to prolong the lifespan of the tracks.

Cabin and Safety Features

  • Regularly inspect and maintain safety equipment, including seat belts, emergency lights, and warning systems.

  • Clean the cabin and ensure that visibility is unobstructed. Check that the controls, air conditioning, and other features are functioning correctly.
