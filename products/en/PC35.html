Maintenance Tips for the Komatsu PC35

To maximize the lifespan and performance of a refurbished Komatsu PC35, it’s essential to perform regular maintenance checks. Proper upkeep not only ensures the machine runs efficiently but also helps avoid costly repairs.

  • Hydraulic Maintenance: Regularly check the hydraulic fluid levels and inspect hoses for any signs of wear or leaks. Change filters as needed and ensure that the hydraulic system is free from contaminants.

  • Engine Care: Change the engine oil regularly, monitor coolant levels, and clean the air filter to maintain optimal engine performance. Pay attention to the exhaust system to ensure it remains free from blockages or leaks.

  • Track and Undercarriage Inspection: Inspect the tracks, rollers, and sprockets for wear. Keep the undercarriage clean and free from debris, as this can affect the machine's stability and performance on uneven ground.

  • General Inspections: Conduct visual checks for any loose or damaged components. Early detection of problems can prevent more extensive and expensive repairs later.
