Maintenance Tips for the Kobelco SK75 Excavator

Proper maintenance is essential for extending the life of the Kobelco SK75 and keeping it in optimal working condition. Here are some maintenance tips to ensure long-term performance:

Regular Engine and Fluid Checks

  • Change the engine oil and replace oil filters regularly to keep the engine in peak condition.

  • Inspect and clean the air filters to prevent debris from entering the engine, which could lead to overheating and poor performance.

  • Check coolant levels frequently to avoid engine overheating.

Hydraulic System Maintenance

  • Regularly check the hydraulic fluid levels and inspect the hydraulic lines for signs of wear or leakage.

  • Change hydraulic filters at recommended intervals to prevent contamination, which could affect performance and cause system failures.

  • Periodically inspect the hydraulic cylinders for leaks and damage.

Undercarriage Maintenance

  • Inspect the tracks, rollers, and idlers for wear and tear. Replace any damaged or worn-out components to ensure smooth movement and stability.

  • Keep the undercarriage clean to avoid debris buildup, which can cause unnecessary wear on components.

Cabin and Operator Comfort Maintenance

  • Regularly clean and inspect the air conditioning system and heating system to maintain comfort for the operator.

  • Ensure that windows and mirrors are clean and free of cracks to provide clear visibility.
