Maintenance Tips for the Komatsu PC440 Excavator

Maintaining a Komatsu PC440 in good working condition is crucial for ensuring its longevity and performance. Here are some maintenance tips:

Engine and Hydraulic System Maintenance

  • Change oil and filters regularly to keep the engine running smoothly.

Undercarriage and Tracks
