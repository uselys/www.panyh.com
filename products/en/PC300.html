Maintenance Tips for the Komatsu PC300 Excavator

Proper maintenance is essential to ensure the longevity and performance of the Komatsu PC300. Below are a few key maintenance tips:

Engine and Hydraulic System

  • Regular Oil Changes: Replace engine oil and hydraulic fluid at recommended intervals to maintain smooth performance and extend the life of the engine.

  • Filter Replacement: Regularly replace filters for both the engine and hydraulic systems to ensure clean, efficient operation.
