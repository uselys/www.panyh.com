What is a Refurbished Excavator

Refurbished Excavators refer to used excavators that undergo systematic inspection and repair to restore them to a condition close to new. Refurbished excavators typically go through full disassembly, cleaning, component replacement, painting, and functional testing to ensure high standards in performance, appearance, and reliability.

Refurbishment Process

Our company has a complete refurbishment workshop and process, with all work conducted internally. If you require third-party inspection, we welcome it.

  1. Comprehensive Inspection
    Technicians assess the engine, hydraulic system, undercarriage, electronics, and machine structure to determine which components can continue to be used and which need replacement.

  2. Power and Hydraulic System Repair
    Engines may require injector replacement, cylinder cleaning, or turbocharger repair; hydraulic pumps, valves, seals, and hoses are refurbished or replaced.

  3. Undercarriage Renewal
    Tracks, rollers, sprockets, and idlers are the most commonly worn parts and are usually replaced during refurbishment.

  4. Cabin and Control System
    Seats, joysticks, display panels, and wiring harnesses are repaired or upgraded to improve operator comfort and safety, and modern monitoring systems may be added.

  5. Appearance Treatment
    Through sanding, rust removal, painting, and protective coatings, the machine looks renewed and resists future corrosion.

  6. Performance Testing
    After refurbishment, the machine is tested under load to ensure digging force, swing speed, and hydraulic response meet standard requirements.
