What is a Refurbished Excavator

Refurbished Excavators refer to used excavators that undergo systematic inspection and repair to restore them to a condition close to new. Refurbished excavators typically go through full disassembly, cleaning, component replacement, painting, and functional testing to ensure high standards in performance, appearance, and reliability.

Many companies don't distinguish between "used" and "refurbished"; they use "used" and "refurbished" interchangeably. Refurbishment Process

Our company has a complete refurbishment workshop and process, with all work conducted internally. If you require third-party inspection, we welcome it.

  1. Comprehensive Inspection
    Technicians assess the engine, hydraulic system, undercarriage, electronics, and machine structure to determine which components can continue to be used and which need replacement.

  2. Power and Hydraulic System Repair
    Engines may require injector replacement, cylinder cleaning, or turbocharger repair; hydraulic pumps, valves, seals, and hoses are refurbished or replaced.

  3. Undercarriage Renewal
    Tracks, rollers, sprockets, and idlers are the most commonly worn parts and are usually replaced during refurbishment.

  4. Cabin and Control System
    Seats, joysticks, display panels, and wiring harnesses are repaired or upgraded to improve operator comfort and safety, and modern monitoring systems may be added.

  5. Appearance Treatment
    Through sanding, rust removal, painting, and protective coatings, the machine looks renewed and resists future corrosion.

  6. Performance Testing
    After refurbishment, the machine is tested under load to ensure digging force, swing speed, and hydraulic response meet standard requirements.

EPA & Stage 5

EPA

1. Exporting as Used Equipment

Principle:

The U.S. Environmental Protection Agency (EPA) has relatively lenient requirements for importing used equipment, focusing primarily on preventing the import of “polluting new machines.”

Key Conditions:

  • The equipment must have been previously used in China or another country, with documented operating hours.
  • Customs declarations and commercial invoices must clearly state: “Used Construction Machinery.”
  • The importer must declare that the equipment is intended solely for personal use, maintenance, or exhibition—not for sale as new machinery.

Risks:

Customs authorities at various ports and states—especially Los Angeles Port—may randomly inspect emission labels and engine models. If the equipment is found to be non-Tier 4 compliant, it may be denied entry or returned.

2. Interstate Differences (Example: Florida)

  • The EPA is a federal agency, and its standards are consistent nationwide.
  • However, enforcement strictness varies by state:
    • California (CARB): The strictest enforcement; importing non-Tier 4 equipment is nearly impossible.
    • Texas and Florida: More relaxed, especially regarding “used equipment” and “agricultural machinery.”
    • Alaska, Guam, and other territories: Offer broader exemptions.

Practical Tip:

Many exporters choose Miami Port or Houston Port as entry points due to more flexible local enforcement.

Stage 5

Exporting as Used Equipment

If the machine has already been used in China, it can be exported as used equipment.

  • Some EU countries have more relaxed emission requirements for used machinery.
    In such cases, only an emission declaration is required, and Stage V certification is not mandatory.
  • The invoice and bill of lading must clearly state “used machinery”, and a technical inspection report should be attached.

Exporting via Non-Road or Special-Purpose Channels

This applies to equipment such as:

  • Agricultural machinery
  • Mining equipment
  • Military vehicles
  • Port handling machinery
  • Forestry equipment

Such equipment may be legally exported by applying for the NRMM exemption (Non-Road Mobile Machinery exemption).

Installing DPF + SCR Systems and Re-Certification

Machines can be retrofitted and tested by third-party laboratories to meet Stage V standards.

  • The cost is relatively high, with each machine potentially requiring an additional investment of €10,000–€30,000.
  • A valid emission certificate must be obtained in accordance with EU Regulation (EU) 2016/1628.

Conclusion

You should confirm the following with customs:

  1. Whether only the engine is being audited. Whether the DFP is effective.
  2. Whether there are differences between new and used engines.
  3. Whether an excavator can be declared for agricultural use and receive an EPA or Stage 5 exemption.

FAQ

Q: Should I buy a used excavator or a refurbished one?
A: If you have the ability to repair and maintain excavators on your own, there’s no doubt that a used excavator is the better choice. You can handle the repairs and adjustments yourself. However, if labor and parts costs in your area are high, a refurbished excavator is the better option. In China, labor and parts costs are much lower. A refurbished machine will give you an experience almost equivalent to a new one.

Q: Why do refurbished excavators typically show around 500 hours of usage?
A: This is a common practice and the current reality in China's used heavy equipment industry, and we are powerless to change it. To distinguish them from new excavators, those with operating hours between 1000 and 4000 hours are set to 500 hours after refurbishment. Excavators with over 5000 hours are not worth refurbishing due to performance decline. Refurbished machines come with warranties, starting from the 500-hour mark, similar to a new machine.

Q: The salesperson told me this is a "stock machine." Is that true?
A: This is a sales pitch commonly used in China, but it’s highly unlikely that there are "stock machines" in the true sense. Even an old machine could never be sold at the price of a refurbished one.

Q: I need to use the excavator in a harsh environment. Can you recommend a suitable one for me?
A: The key issue isn't with the excavator itself—designers and sellers alike hope it will operate reliably. However, any excavator will encounter problems quickly in harsh environments. Therefore, the real key is having skilled technicians and experienced operators. Typically, construction companies employ repair technicians who regularly check the excavator for faults.

Q: Should I pay a deposit online?
A: If it’s for a long-term cooperation, this can be feasible. However, if it’s your first purchase, paying a deposit online is a high-risk move. Many Chinese companies use deposits as a major income source. Once the deposit is received, they gain the upper hand.

Q: Can I use the excavator directly after it arrives?
A: It depends on the size of the excavator. If your excavator doesn’t fit directly into the container, unfortunately, we’ll have to disassemble it and pack it into the container. You will need to reassemble it on your end.

Q: How is your spoken English?
A: We typically use a translator for communication, as very few people in this industry are fluent in English. If you have a WeChat account, its built-in translator is quite convenient for use.

Q: Is it really only 3 hours from Shanghai to Hefei by high-speed train?
A: It indeed takes around 3 hours from Shanghai Railway Station to Hefei Railway Station, but that's under ideal conditions. In reality, if you factor in transfer times, the total journey can take 6-7 hours. So, if you’re carrying a lot of luggage, it’s advisable to take a car directly.

Q: Which parts are replaced during a refurbishment? Are these original parts?
A: Rubber pipes and consumable parts. The main parts are usually cleaned rather than replaced. The parts that will not be replaced include the excavator body, engine, hydraulic pump, hydraulic distributor, and main control circuit system.

Q: All refurbishing work done in brand service centre or repair by yourself?
A: The repair center only services machines that are under warranty. The warranty period in China is 1000 hours.

Q: In refurbishing you change all parts was new and original from brand?
A: They are brand new. Usually, only the easily worn-out parts are replaced. They all come from reputable manufacturers, and their quality is comparable to the original parts.

Q: Are you an authorized dealer?
A: No, I'm not. The official warranty for the used equipment has already expired. Therefore, we do not need to obtain authorization. Furthermore, the brands of used equipment are very diverse. If we were to obtain authorization for every brand, we would likely become the world's largest used equipment dealer, employing hundreds of people, which would significantly increase costs, and the used excavators would also be sold at a much higher price.
